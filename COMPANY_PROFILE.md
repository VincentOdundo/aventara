## 01 · The company

**A Nairobi operator working both sides of the border.**

We plan and run road safaris through East Africa's best-known parks. Every trip starts in Nairobi. The shortest is a half-day outing in the city; the longest is 12 days across both countries.

Our safaris are built for travellers watching their budget who still want the real thing: a pop-up-roof 4x4, a window seat, a professional driver-guide, and a price with the park fees already in it.

**Kenya:** Masai Mara · Amboseli · Lake Nakuru · Lake Naivasha · Hell's Gate · Nairobi National Park
**Tanzania:** Serengeti · Ngorongoro Crater · Tarangire · Lake Manyara
