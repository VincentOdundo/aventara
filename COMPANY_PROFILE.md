## 01 · The company

**A Nairobi operator working both sides of the border.**

We plan and run road safaris through East Africa's best-known parks. Every trip starts in Nairobi. The shortest is a half-day outing in the city; the longest is 12 days across both countries.

Our safaris are built for travellers watching their budget who still want the real thing: a pop-up-roof 4x4, a window seat, a professional driver-guide, and a price with the park fees already in it.

**Kenya:** Masai Mara · Amboseli · Lake Nakuru · Lake Naivasha · Hell's Gate · Nairobi National Park
**Tanzania:** Serengeti · Ngorongoro Crater · Tarangire · Lake Manyara

2. **Send your dates.** Message us on WhatsApp or by email with your travel dates and the number of travellers.

## 06 · Good to know

- **Cancellation.** Free cancellation up to 30 days before departure.
- **Children.** Children under 4 travel free. Under-18s get reduced rates on multi-day Kenya safaris.
- **Solo travellers.** Single-room rates are published next to the sharing rate. Where none is listed, ask and we will quote.
- **Tipping.** Tips are not included. Our guideline is US$10 per person per day for guides and camp staff.
- **Park fees.** Current park fees and taxes are in the price. If a government raises them after you book, the difference is charged at cost.
- **Support.** Our WhatsApp desk is staffed around the clock before and during your trip.
